InvManager privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

InvManager (TikTok Shop app name: warehouseShop) is a private tool used by one small UK shop's owners and staff to manage their own inventory and fulfil their own TikTok Shop orders. The shop is the data controller for the personal data described here. This policy is reviewed at least once a year.

What Google data it uses

  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets scope): to read and write the shop's own inventory spreadsheet.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): to upload product and sale photos to a folder the app created. It can only see files it created itself, not the rest of your Drive.

What TikTok Shop data it uses

  • Orders from our own shop: order ID, items, price, buyer TikTok username, recipient name, delivery address and phone number.
  • Our own live streams: public chat comments and usernames, to record who won each auction.

We use it only to match auction wins to orders, pack and ship them, handle returns and keep tax and accounting records. The lawful bases under UK GDPR are performance of a contract (fulfilling the order) and legal obligation (tax records).

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to keep the shop's inventory and sales records up to date and to fulfil orders.
  • Data is stored in the United Kingdom, on the shop's own server (DigitalOcean, London) and the shop's own computers.
  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access is limited to the owner and named staff.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The only exceptions are the carriers TikTok Shop assigns to deliver a parcel, and the providers that host our systems (DigitalOcean, Cloudflare, Google) under their data protection terms.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

  • Order data is kept for up to 6 years for UK tax and accounting records, then deleted.
  • Server backups are kept for 14 days.
  • If we stop using TikTok Shop's API, the customer data we received through it is deleted within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export, delete or restrict your personal data, or object to how we use it. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Removing access

You can revoke InvManager's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files it created in your Drive and Sheets stay yours and can be deleted normally.
