InvManager privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

InvManager is a private tool used by one small shop's owners and staff to manage their own inventory.

What Google data it uses

  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets scope): to read and write the shop's own inventory spreadsheet.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): to upload product and sale photos to a folder the app created. It can only see files it created itself, not the rest of your Drive.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to keep the shop's inventory and sales records up to date.
  • The login token and a local copy of the inventory are stored on the shop's own computer or server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke InvManager's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files it created in your Drive and Sheets stay yours and can be deleted normally.
